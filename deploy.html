04

Next steps after deploying

  1. Check the address. Open the contract on BscScan (link in step 02) and confirm it is the CrownRaceGame you just deployed.
  2. Entries stay closed for now. Do not open them in step 03 until Muse confirms the new crownrace.site is live with this contract wired in — otherwise racers could enter a heat before the site shows it.
  3. Send the address to Muse. Copy the summary box below and send it in one paste. Muse wires the address into the site, the copy-ready agent prompts, and llms.txt / agent.json, then re-pushes.
  4. Then open entries in step 03 (owner wallet only) when Muse says the live site is verified.
  5. Announce last. The launch post to Season 1 holders via @PlayCrownRace goes out only after the site is live and entries are open.
