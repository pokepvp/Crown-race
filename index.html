      Enter. Move daily. Take the crown.

      01

      Enter the heat

      When a weekly heat opens, enter with a Rookie racer for free or Pro racers for 0.0007 BNB each. Both lanes open at the same time — Pro is never locked behind the free lane.

      02

      Move every day

      Every entered racer can move once per day for free. Each move advances 1–6 steps, drawn on-chain. Show up daily — consistency beats luck over 100 steps. Optional Nitro buys extra steps instantly.

      03

      Win at the settle

      The heat ends the moment someone crosses step 100, or on Monday 00:00 UTC. Anyone can call the settle — the contract pays the winner 50% of the pool automatically and crowns them in the Hall of Champions.

      The daily move & Nitro

      One free move a day keeps you in the race

      The daily move is the core loop of Crown Race — and it is free, forever, for every racer. Nitro exists only to go faster.

      Daily move — 0 BNB

      Once per day, each of your racers can move. The move advances your racer by 1 to 6 steps. It costs 0 BNB, so an AI agent can call it for you automatically every day — set it up once and never miss a day.

      123456

      The step count is drawn on-chain from block data at the moment you move. The draw is written into your transaction, so anyone can recompute it afterwards and check that your steps were fair.

      Honest note: this is verifiable randomness from block data — transparent and checkable by anyone after the fact, but it is not VRF-grade randomness. We say so plainly instead of dressing it up.

      FAQ

      Questions, answered straight

      What is Crown Race?

      Crown Race is an on-chain racing game on BNB Chain. Racers advance along a 100-step track toward a golden crown in weekly heats. You enter a racer, move it forward once a day for free (1–6 steps per move), optionally buy Nitro for instant extra steps, and race everyone else to step 100. The winner of each heat receives 50% of that heat's prize pool, paid automatically by the smart contract.

      Who can race?

      Anyone whose wallet holds a Binance Agent ID (.ai NFT). The Agent ID check happens on-chain at entry, for both lanes: Rookie allows 1 racer per wallet per heat, Pro allows up to 3. Entries per heat are capped at 2,222 racers in total.

      What is the difference between Rookie and Pro entry?

      Price, quantity, and starting position. Rookie is free (0 BNB), 1 racer per wallet, starting at step 0. Pro costs 0.0007 BNB per racer, up to 3 racers per wallet, and each Pro racer starts 5 steps ahead, at step 5. Everything after entry is identical: the same free daily move, the same Nitro prices, the same chance at the same prize. Both lanes open in parallel from the first minute of the heat — Pro is never locked behind the free lane.

      How does the daily move work — and how are the steps drawn?

      Once per day, every entered racer can call the move function for free (0 BNB) — an AI agent can do this automatically. Each move advances the racer 1–6 steps. The step count is drawn on-chain from block data at the moment of the move, and because it is recorded in the transaction, anyone can recompute it afterwards and verify the draw was fair. To be fully honest: block-data randomness is verifiable, but it is not VRF-grade randomness — we will not pretend otherwise.

      What is Nitro? Do I need it to win?

      Nitro is an optional paid boost that moves your racer forward instantly: +2 steps for 0.00015 BNB, +7 steps for 0.0005 BNB, or +20 steps for 0.0015 BNB. You never need it — the free daily move is the core loop, and over a 7-day heat a racer who moves daily (7–42 free steps) plus the Pro head start can go far. Nitro is for comebacks and final-stretch fights. All Nitro payments go into the heat prize pool.

      How and when are prizes paid?

      Automatically, by the smart contract, when the heat settles. A heat runs Monday 00:00 UTC to Monday 00:00 UTC and ends early the moment a racer crosses step 100. Settling is permissionless — anyone can call it — and in that same transaction the contract sends 50% of the pool to the winner and 50% to the project. The pool is funded by Pro entries and Nitro purchases. Winner = first finisher, or farthest along at heat end; ties go to whoever reached that step first. No manual payouts and no promises outside the contract.

      When does Crown Race launch?

      Launch date: to be announced. The contract is not deployed yet, entry is not open, and there is no official contract address anywhere — treat any address claiming to be Crown Race before our announcement as fake. The first heat opens at launch; follow @PlayCrownRace on X for the announcement.

      Is there a Crown Race token?

      No. Crown Race has no token and no plans for one. The game runs on BNB entry fees and Nitro only, and winners are paid in BNB by the contract.
